At each run, use the current date to establish the target season, then inspect **only 同桌英语**:

Do not ask the learner to search for or paste a topic bank. The page can change. Record its stated season, update date if shown, access date, and whether it presents a complete Part 2 list or only a preview. Extract Part 2 cue-card subjects and available prompts; exclude Part 1 and Part 3. Keep a source URL for each question. Deduplicate equivalent English and Chinese titles while retaining materially different cue-card constraints and prompt wording.

The plain-text page may show template placeholders rather than topics. In a rendered browser, the publicly visible page has a **模块** row with Part1, Part2, and Part3, a **新旧** row, and a **主题** row. Select **Part2**, leave **新旧 = 全部** and **主题 = 全部**, and read the resulting topic cards, including cards below the first viewport. Check the visible season label against the current date. Record the number of unique cards actually read and their titles. Do not stop after the first screen or collect only “本季新题”; the current list can also include retained topics.

For **each** listed Part 2 topic, click its topic label/card and verify that the question-detail view opened; then read the full English cue-card wording and every visible “You should say” bullet. Record the title, full prompt, source page, and any constraint that changes the answer. Do not infer from the list view that opening the question requires login. If a click leaves the page unchanged, inspect the current UI and try another normal visible route to the same detail; use another supported browser if available and useful. A click attempt alone is not evidence that the prompt was read.

Only after normal access attempts fail for a particular question, mark it **title only** and infer no hidden “You should say” bullets. Map title-only cards cautiously and mark unseen constraints as unknown. Do not bypass a real login or payment gate to obtain answers or audio.

If the site cannot supply a complete current-season list through authorized access, continue with its verified, accessible Part 2 topics. Report coverage as “X/Y accessible, deduplicated 同桌英语 Part 2 topics gathered on [date]” rather than “all current-season topics” when completeness remains uncertain. Distinguish title-only topics from topics with full cue-card wording in the coverage report. Do not add topics from another website, fabricate inaccessible questions, or send the learner to collect the bank.
